## 🧩 About

**Atomfall Companion** is a curated list of Atomfall open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, loot markers, waypoint viewer
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — inventory notes, crafting references, map overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — survival routes and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Survival Helpers
- Waypoint Notes — mark and review points of interest
- Loot Overlay — visualize nearby loot categories
- Map Notes — study the quarantine zone layout
- Playback Speed — adjust menu pacing for study

### 🎨 Inventory & Crafting
- Inventory Catalog — browse items, recipes, and materials
- Crafting Notes — organize supply recipes
- HUD Tuning — improved readability for stats

### 🏋️ Research Layout
- Route Notes — segment study across regions
- Combat Pacing Notes — review encounters in the field
- Config Presets — chain settings for study sessions
